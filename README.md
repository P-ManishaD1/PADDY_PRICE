# 🌾 Profitable Market and Price Approaches for Paddy

**Empowering Farmers with Real-Time Paddy Price Insights**

This project aims to assist farmers by providing up-to-date market prices for different types of paddy across various locations. By comparing market trends and analyzing profitable selling points, farmers can make better-informed decisions to maximize their income.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To build a web-based platform that:
- Displays daily and weekly paddy prices.
- Compares prices across different local and regional markets.
- Helps farmers identify the most profitable market to sell their crops.
- Enhances transparency and market awareness in the agriculture sector.

---

## 🔍 Features

- 📈 **Live Price Updates** from multiple marketplaces.
- 📊 **Market Comparison Tool** to find the highest prices.
- 🧠 **Trend Analysis** for past days/weeks.
- 🗺️ **Location-based Price Search**.
- 🌐 **Multilingual Support** for rural accessibility.
- 🧑‍🌾 **Farmer-Friendly Interface** with simple navigation.
